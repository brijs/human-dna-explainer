# Genomes & Proteins: storyboard (adult level, three.js, guide: Dr. Helix; ranks Rookie → Analyst → Specialist → Frontier)

| # | Scene | What you do | Takeaway |
|---|-------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | The Map | Click nodes of a 3D concept graph; jump to scenes | Genome → regulation → amino acids → structure → folding → AlphaFold → open problems |
| 2 | Genome at scale | 3D bar chart of 24 chromosomes; switch length / genes / density | Size ≠ gene count; T2T 3.055 Gb |
| 3 | What's in 3 billion letters | 3D pie, three "function" lenses | ~1.5% coding, ~50% repeats; function debate |
| 4 | Variation | Log-scale 3D bars: you vs stranger/Neanderthal/chimp; inside one genome | ~0.1% differences; SVs cover more bases than SNPs |
| 5 | Regulation | Loop enhancers to promoters in a 3D chromatin chain; add methylation | Same genome, different expression |
| 6 | Twenty building blocks | 3D property space (hydropathy × volume), peptide builder | Hydrophobicity drives folding |
| 7 | Four levels of structure | Real hemoglobin: fold slider, levels, residue picking, zoom to atoms | Primary → quaternary |
| 8 | Levinthal & the funnel | Paradox calculator; 3D energy landscape with a rolling ball, 40-ball experiment, annealing | Funnels make folding fast |
| 9 | Hydrophobic collapse | Live physics toy: release, poke beads, temperature, multi-chain aggregation | Core formation vs aggregation |
| 10 | When folding goes wrong | Sickle β6 Glu→Val on real 3D hemoglobin; build an amyloid fibril | Misfolding diseases |
| 11 | The prediction problem | 3D timeline 1958–2024, sequences vs structures, GDT | Why prediction mattered |
| 12 | How AlphaFold works | Contact map ↔ 3D, predicted vs experimental overlay (Kabsch RMSD), pLDDT on p53/α-synuclein | Co-evolution → structure; confidence |
| 13 | Design & drugs | Pull indinavir out of HIV protease, count contacts; Top7 de novo design | Structure-based drugs; Nobel 2024 |
| 14 | Open problems | Frontier map (8 problems) + live demos: dynamics (T↔R morph), disorder, membrane | What nobody can do yet |
| 15 | Quiz + cheat sheet | 8 questions, 6+ for Frontier rank; resources and sources | Where to go next |

Data: real structures from RCSB PDB (1UBQ, 2HHB, 1HHO, 1HSG, 1QYS, 1C3W) and AlphaFold DB (P04637, P37840, P68871); see `genome/pack_data.py`.
