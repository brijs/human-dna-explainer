# Human DNA Explainer — Storyboard (guide: Dr. Helix the robot, bio-lab neon)

| # | Level | Scene | What the learner does | Takeaway |
|---|-------|-------|-----------------------|----------|
| 1 | Cadet | Welcome to the Lab | Drag to spin a glowing 3D helix, meet Dr. Helix | Your body follows a recipe written in DNA |
| 2 | Cadet | Zoom Into a Cell | Slide a zoom control through body → cell → nucleus → chromosome → DNA with real sizes | DNA lives in the nucleus, packed inside chromosomes |
| 3 | Cadet | Packing 2 Metres of DNA | Slider coils a 3D DNA strand: helix → nucleosome beads → fibre → X chromosome | Histones spool DNA so it fits |
| 4 | Scientist | 23 Pairs: Karyotype | Click matching chromosomes to pair them; toggle XX / XY | 46 chromosomes = 23 pairs, one set from each parent |
| 5 | Scientist | The Double Helix | Rotate 3D helix, click parts (backbone, bases, turn) | Twisted ladder, 10 base pairs per turn, 2 nm wide |
| 6 | Scientist | Base-Pair Builder | Build the matching strand A-T, C-G; watch it twist in 3D | Pairing rules let DNA copy itself |
| 7 | Scientist | Genes: Recipes in the Book | Click the gene regions on a 3D chromosome; see traits | ~20,000 genes, only ~1–2% of DNA codes for protein |
| 8 | Researcher | DNA → RNA → Protein | Edit DNA, watch mRNA and codons build amino acids | Codons (3 letters) spell amino acids |
| 9 | Researcher | Mutation Lab | Apply silent / missense / nonsense / deletion; sickle-cell example | Small DNA changes can change or not change a protein |
| 10 | Researcher | Inheritance & Punnett | Cross alleles, flip XX/XY, calico-cat puzzle | Alleles, dominance, sex chromosomes |
| 11 | Researcher | Humans & Other Species | Click animals: chromosome count, DNA shared with humans, trait links | Shared genes show shared ancestry |
| 12 | Researcher → Master | Final Quiz + Cheat Sheet | 8-question quiz (6+ passes), copy cheat sheet | Passing unlocks Master rank |
